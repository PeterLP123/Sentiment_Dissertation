What This Phase Is

The repository already contains a hardened pipeline, nine registered experiments, four frozen tail-risk cells, and a long trail of honest nulls. What it does not contain is a single clean body of work that answers one question well. This phase builds that.

The strategy is deliberately narrow: fix the measurement, not the trading rule. Every trading result so far has failed or been inconclusive, and the diagnostics point at the same culprit each time - the signal is a mean of tone over a badly filtered set of stories, computed on a company-day grain that is unstable. So the final work concentrates on how stories are selected, weighted, and combined, and treats the portfolio layer as a downstream sanity check rather than the object of study.

The output is notebooks, figures and tables that a reader can follow, not another CLI surface.

Core rule for this phase

Aggregate across stories, not across scorers. The signal-portfolio study already showed that combining scorers monotonically destroys Sharpe here (1.274 at k=1 down to 0.630 at k=7), and that the winner is indistinguishable from selection luck. FinBERT is the scorer. The open question is what to do with the many stories it scores.

Workstream 2

Which Stories Deserve Weight

Goal. A defensible filter and weighting scheme, plus the exploratory analysis of the within-company-day score distribution that the rest of the plan depends on.

Publisher weighting

There is no prior art here - the repo filters and splits by source but has never weighted one. Available structure: source_code with 2,854 distinct values in the sector-33 corpus (largest single source NS:GLORDP at 16.0%; Reuters NS:RTRS is 23,191 of 710,625 rows), and per-site row and symbol counts for FNSPID. Note the one measured data point: separating Reuters from non-Reuters produced two signals correlated 0.99 with the parent, and the Reuters-only arm had a negative gross Sharpe of -0.449. Weighting by prestige is a hypothesis, not a known win.

Breaking news vs repetition vs routine reporting

Three-way classification. Existing ingredients: story_family_id revision chains, exact and normalised headline hashes, a market-recap regex that flags 9.86% of FNSPID headlines, the 12-type event taxonomy, and an LLM-scored five-level novelty label validated against strictly-earlier same-company headlines. New candidates: first-mention flag, near-duplicate distance against the firm's trailing 30-day headline corpus, revision count within family, and time-since-last- story-for-this-firm.

Workstream 3

Aggregation Rules

Goal. Compare ways of collapsing many story scores into one firm-day signal, on one panel, one split, one inference procedure. This is the most likely home for the primary RQ.

The current production rule everywhere in this repo is sign of the mean hard label - build_event_labels and MeanSignalBuilder both average -1/0/+1 and trade the sign. Nothing has ever been tested against it on a level playing field.

Rule Definition Why it is on the list
Mean hard label Mean of -1/0/+1, trade the sign. The incumbent. The baseline everything is measured against.
Mean continuous Mean of p_pos - p_neg. Keeps magnitude; already computed in every score file.
Median Median continuous score. Robust to one extreme headline dominating a high-n day.
Trimmed mean Mean after dropping the top and bottom 10%. Middle ground between mean and median; needs n to be meaningful.
Negative share Fraction of stories labelled negative. The only text feature with a strongly significant, BH-corrected effect anywhere in this repo (t = 5.19 on next-session |AR|).
Dispersion Std or IQR of same-day scores. Disagreement within a day is a natural uncertainty proxy and connects to the standing agreement RQ.
Strongest event Single story per firm-day, ranked by materiality, novelty, then |score|; opposite-direction ties excluded. Already implemented in directional_event_gate.py; never benchmarked against the mean.
Attention weighted Score weighted by log(1 + n), or by publisher tier, or by novelty class. Volume is the one thing that decisively mattered for tail risk; it should be tested on the first moment too.
Decayed state Half-life decay with severity impulse and reversal reset, from state.py. Exists and is tuned; belongs in the comparison as the multi-day memory arm.
Workstream 4

Sentiment Surprise, Properly Controlled

Goal. Separate what is genuinely new in a firm's sentiment from the market-wide sentiment level and the firm's own habitual tone. The July pilot did this once and recorded a NO-GO; this is a redesign, not a repeat.

What went wrong last time, and what must change

  • Only 28 evaluation date clusters. The new design must use the wide-panel spine, where the tail-risk work had 1,758 target dates.
  • Market effect stripped as a raw difference (stock minus ^GSPC), with no fitted market model. Use the alpha, beta market model already implemented in l2_event_study.py.
  • Only the firm's own history was removed. The cross-sectional daily mean sentiment was never removed, so "surprise" still contained market-wide tone.
  • Three evaluation runs, two invalidated. Disclose the run count and the reason for each invalidation in whatever is written.

The demeaning stack

Peel the signal in a declared order, and report the incremental effect of each layer rather than only the final residual:

  1. Raw firm-day sentiment.
  2. Minus the firm's trailing baseline (EWMA or trailing-k-news-day mean, strictly excluding today).
  3. Minus the cross-sectional daily mean across the panel - the market sentiment level.
  4. Optionally minus a sector-day mean, to separate firm-specific from sector news.

On the return side, strip the market with a fitted market model over a declared estimation window rather than a raw difference, and keep the initial price reaction as a separate regressor so surprise is not just re-reading the event-day move.

Cross-cutting

Learned Thresholds

Every strategy in this repo gates on a fixed symmetric band - no_trade_band = 0.10 with a [0.0, 0.10, 0.20] grid, or threshold quantiles, or VADER's +/-0.05. A learned threshold - possibly per stock or per sector - is worth one honest attempt.

Framing

Learn a gate, not a return forecast: map firm-day features (score moments, story count, dispersion, publisher mix, novelty class, event type, trailing volatility, sector) to a trade / no-trade decision or an expected-|return| threshold.

Sample constraint

The LSEG development window is about 85 sessions. A neural net there is guaranteed overfitting. This arm only makes sense on the wide FNSPID spine, and with a linear or gradient-boosted baseline reported beside it.

Honesty requirement

Fit development-only, no evaluation peeking, seed recorded, and the comparison is always against the fixed band. If the fixed band wins, that is the finding and it strengthens the paper.

How We Would Fool Ourselves

Forking paths

Nine aggregation rules, twelve event types, several horizons and two spines is a large implicit search. Declare each family before looking and apply Benjamini-Hochberg. The signal-portfolio study is the cautionary tale: its winner was beaten by 52.2% of a demeaned null.

Selection on coverage

Firms and days only enter the panel when they have news and a tradable price. That is a collider. State it, and check whether results differ between high-coverage and low-coverage firms.

Model labels as truth

Novelty, materiality and event type are all model- or regex-derived. Treating them as ground truth is an assumption, not a measurement - the repo already has a written record of exactly this decision. Audit samples are the mitigation.

Reused samples

Both candidate spines have already influenced design. Nothing here can be called confirmatory. Use "chronological evaluation block" and mean it.

Turnover blindness

A gross result that dies at 10 bps per side is not a result. Every arm reports turnover and break-even cost. The 33-stock run reached 405.6x annualised turnover and a break-even of 3.31 bps.

Scope creep back into VaR

Tail risk is answered and the machinery is inviting. It is out of scope. Cite the factorial and move on.

Out Of Scope

  • New VaR / ES work. The factorial is finished: arrival matters, semantics do not, and the effect runs through intensity rather than direction.
  • New model bake-offs. FinBERT wins on the labelled benchmark and hosted LLMs did not beat it. Scorer choice is settled.
  • Multi-scorer ensembles. Enumerated and measured: they lose here.
  • Learned text weights in the Ke-Kelly-Xiu style. Already deferred as too data-hungry for these cohorts.
  • Intraday execution, borrow, spread and market-impact modelling. Carry the existing limitation text; do not build it.
  • Reuters symbol enrichment for FNSPID. Needs a point-in-time security master; already deferred as a separate validated extension.
  • New CLI subcommands, frozen manifests, or pipeline stages unless a result is being promoted into the dissertation.
