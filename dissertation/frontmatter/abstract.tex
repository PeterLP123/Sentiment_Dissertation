Sentiment trading pipelines compress each day's financial news into a
single scalar score and trade on it. This dissertation argues the
scalar is not a sufficient statistic: when multiple models, prompts,
and samples read the same text, the distribution of their readings
carries economically meaningful information the scalar destroys.
