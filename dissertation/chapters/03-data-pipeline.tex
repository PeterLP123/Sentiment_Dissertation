\section{Model Roster and Scoring}
\label{sec:data-roster}

% The roster freeze is a feature — state it as one.
[The frozen roster: 4--5 hosted \glspl{llm} via OpenRouter, 1--2 local
models via Ollama, plus FinBERT \parencite{araci2019finbert} and
\gls{vader} \parencite{hutto2014vader} as non-LLM contrast. Roster was
frozen before formal runs; additions would require re-running
everything. Each item is scored by every roster member under every
prompt variant with $k$ stochastic samples --- this crossed design is
what makes the L3 G-study possible. Per-run provenance: prompt hashes
and \texttt{commit\_sha} in the experiment manifest (full prompts in
\cref{app:prompts}).]

\section{Aggregation}
\label{sec:data-aggregation}

[Article scores $\to$ daily $S_t$: weighting, the after-close
assignment rule, weekend handling. The scalar built here is L1's input
--- and the information it destroys is L2/L3's subject, so document
the compression precisely.]

\begin{equation}
  S_t = \frac{1}{N_t} \sum_{i=1}^{N_t} s_{i,t},
  \label{eq:daily-sentiment}
\end{equation}
where $s_{i,t}$ is the (roster-mean) score of article $i$ mapped to
trading day $t$ and $N_t$ is the article count.

\section{Leakage Controls}
\label{sec:data-leakage}

% This section gets its own number because three chapters cite it.
[The news-before-decision timestamp rule; chronological splits
everywhere; no test-window tuning (threshold $L$ and sizing parameters
tuned on train only); the memorization caveat for pre-cutoff text
\parencite{glasserman2023lookahead}, acknowledged rather than claimed
solved.]

\section{Rigor Protocol}
\label{sec:data-rigor}

[Pre-registered hypotheses H2a--c and H3a--c with horizons fixed in
advance (1, 5, 10 days); Benjamini--Hochberg correction across the
hypothesis family \parencite{benjamini1995fdr} --- the
multiple-testing critique of \textcite{harvey2016multiple} is the
reason; soft-label scoring with Brier/\gls{ece} evaluated before any
formal run; transaction-cost sensitivity on every P/L claim; AI use
restricted to code and analysis, logged.]
