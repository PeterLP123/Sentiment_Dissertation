\section{Research archive}

The research archive contains the LaTeX source, selected notebooks, analysis
code, analysis plans, aggregate results and licence-safe figures. Raw news text,
row-level datasets, API responses, model caches and credentials are excluded.
Full replication requires authorised access to the original workspace and
licensed inputs.

Saved summaries, analysis plans, hashes and seeds link the reported results to
their source files. The fixed 2020--2023 estimates were calculated once under the
recorded rule and were not rerun during manuscript preparation. Other analyses
write to separate output directories and cannot overwrite them.

Before compilation, reproducibility checks regenerated 30 figures, tables and
supporting files from saved aggregate inputs.

\section{Artificial-intelligence assistance}

I used OpenAI Codex to assist with code, figures, source checks, drafting and
editing. I checked its output against the saved results and cited sources and
remain responsible for the dissertation.
