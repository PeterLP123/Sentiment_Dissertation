The whole system

Seven frozen stages

The working hypothesis is short-lived underreaction to company-specific news. Text becomes a trade only after it survives source, timestamp, target and market-data checks; prices never influence labels, and every stage verifies a manifest.

  1. 1

    Collect

    Reuters-only LSEG queries for 22 named companies, English language, 26 June 2025 to 26 June 2026.

  2. 2

    Canonicalise

    Build story families, preserve revisions and select the earliest usable company-specific occurrence.

  3. 3

    Screen

    Require one explicit target. Remove multi-company and market-price or technical recap headlines.

  4. 4

    Label

    Run pinned FinBERT and a fixed VADER share-argmax comparator locally, without downloads.

  5. 5

    Schedule

    Add 15 minutes to publication availability, then choose the first strictly later XNYS open.

  6. 6

    Trade

    Build equal-weight long and short legs. Hold from one adjusted open to the next. No carry.

  7. 7

    Evaluate

    Charge turnover costs, force liquidation and use a five-session moving-block bootstrap.

Market layer

The trading rule

The rule targets immediate cross-sectional drift: use only information available before entry, turn same-stock same-session labels into one sign, require a genuine two-sided portfolio and close at the next adjusted open rather than assuming sentiment persists.

Processing buffer

A frozen operational delay prevents instantaneous execution assumptions.

Book one open-to-open return. No news means no carried signal. Final positions are liquidated.

Frozen signal

label ∈ {−1, 0, +1}
si,d = sign(mean(labelsi,d))
wlong = +0.50 / Nlong
wshort = −0.50 / Nshort
A session stays in zero-return cash unless it contains at least one long and one short. Gross target exposure is 1.0 and net target exposure is 0.
  1. Aggregate within company and eligible session.Positive maps to +1, neutral to 0 and negative to −1. Trade only the sign of the mean.
  2. Build equal-weight legs.Every positive company shares the +50% long leg. Every negative company shares the −50% short leg.
  3. Require both sides.No one-sided fallback. If either leg is empty, the whole portfolio remains in cash.
  4. Hold exactly one session.Entry is the first eligible adjusted open; exit is the following XNYS adjusted open. Signals do not carry.
  5. Charge every buy and sell.Cost = 10 bp × Σ|target weight − current weight|. An unchanged position’s open-and-close round trip therefore costs about 20 bp.
  6. Keep evaluation chronological.The interpretation boundary is 2026-01-02. Terminal liquidation remains an audit row but is folded into the final holding interval for statistics.

Research basis

What comes from the literature

No single paper is reproduced exactly. The baseline combines a short-horizon media signal, finance-specific language modelling and a daily cross-sectional portfolio, while documenting every difference from the closest template.

2007

Tetlock · Giving Content to Investor Sentiment

Journal of Finance

Used: timestamped media pessimism, short-horizon return interpretation and caution around reversal and trading costs. Not copied: WSJ market-level corpus or General Inquirer method.

2011

Loughran & McDonald · When Is a Liability Not a Liability?

Journal of Finance

Used: the reason to prefer finance-domain sentiment over generic language polarity. Not copied: the 10-K dictionary study itself.

2014

Hutto & Gilbert · VADER

ICWSM

Used: a deterministic lexical control. Deviation: this artifact uses share-argmax, not the paper’s canonical compound thresholds.

2019

Araci · FinBERT

arXiv:1908.10063

Used: a finance-domain, three-class local sentiment scorer. Limit: text-classification performance does not prove return predictability.

2025

López-Lira & Tang · Can ChatGPT Forecast Stock Price Movements?

arXiv:2304.07619v6 · closest portfolio template

Used: company-linked headline labels aggregated into daily long and short portfolios with turnover and cost analysis. Adapted: model, universe, timing, gross exposure, leg minimum and cost convention.

2019

NBER Working Paper 26186

Deferred: its return-specific learned text weights need a much larger corpus. Training such a model on this 22-stock year would invite severe overfitting.

Interpretation boundary

What this does not prove

The strategy is useful because it fixes a transparent positive-Sharpe reference point. Its design also places hard limits on what can be claimed from the observed 4.004% evaluation return.

  • Not untouched.The one-year, 22-stock cohort was previously explored. The evaluation date split helps interpretation but does not restore pristine out-of-sample status.
  • Not causal.A historical association between labels and returns does not show that sentiment caused the price movement.
  • Not statistically settled.The 95% moving-block interval includes zero, so the average daily advantage is inconclusive.
  • Not article understanding.Only headlines are scored. Context, novelty, materiality and target-specific expected cash-flow effects are not modelled.
  • Not total-return accounting.Opens are split-adjusted but dividends are not back-adjusted.
  • Not full execution reality.Costs omit varying spreads, market impact, short availability, borrow fees, financing, taxes and capacity.
  • Not interest-bearing cash.The cash comparator earns exactly zero; T-bill yield and cash interest are omitted.
  • Not a reason to retune.Changing thresholds, horizon, cohort or model after seeing the result would turn this sample into an optimisation target.
  • Not universe-general.The unchanged rule lost money on the 33-stock sector cohort, so the positive mid-cap path cannot be described as broad-market alpha.
  • Not investment advice.This is a dissertation research artifact and engineering baseline, not a recommendation to trade.
